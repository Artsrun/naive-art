Legal Setup
Start Here
🪪 1.1 E-Trader Permit — Recommended First Step
  • Visit DED Café (Jumeirah 1, Beach Road) or apply via Dubai Economy portal
  • Prepare: Passport copy, Emirates ID, Good Conduct Certificate (Dubai Police app or MOI), Proof of address
  • Select activity category: "Handicrafts / Art / E-commerce retail"
  • Pay permit fee (≈ AED 1,300–1,500) and submit application
  • Await approval (1–3 weeks processing time)
Cost & Timeline

≈ AED 1,300–1,500 · Processing: 1–3 weeks · Reference: DED official process (2026)

🎨 1.2 Freelance Visa — If Planning Full-Time
  • Choose free zone: Dubai Design District (d3) or Dubai Media City (via GoFreelance.ae)
  • Apply under "Artist / Visual Artist" category
  • Prepare portfolio (min. 10–15 pieces), CV / artist statement, passport + photos, proof of previous art activity
  • Submit application and pay fee (AED 12,000–15,000 for license + 2-year visa + Emirates ID)
References

GoFreelance.ae and d3 official portals · Processing: 4–8 weeks

2
Physical Market Applications
🛍️ 2.1 ARTE – The Makers' Market (Easiest Entry)
  • Email info@arte.ae to express interest
  • Register via "Become a Vendor" form on arte.ae
  • Prepare: photos of current work (min. 8–10 pieces) and short description of your art style
  • Pay AED 125 registration + AED 325+ per event day (min. 2 weeks in advance)
  • Confirm rules: handmade only, no roll-up banners, price list required
Venue

New vendors start at Times Square Center, Al Quoz · 2× per month events

🌍 2.2 Global Village — Higher Volume (Seasonal)
  • Register interest at business.globalvillage.ae
  • Submit concept proposal + business plan
  • Obtain valid UAE trade license (or E-Trader Permit) first
  • Arrange insurance coverage and prepare financial proof
Recommended Timeline

Best to apply after 3–6 months of ARTE experience. High tourist footfall, seasonal program.

3
Tax & VAT Compliance
  • Set up a system to monitor annual turnover against the AED 375,000 VAT threshold
  • Register for VAT only if taxable supplies exceed AED 375,000 in a rolling 12 months
  • Keep simple records: date, item, price, buyer type (UAE vs export)
  • Note: Exports are zero-rated (0%) with proper customs documentation
Current Rate

5% on domestic UAE sales · Reference: UAE Ministry of Finance VAT rules for artists (2026)

4
Online Sales Setup
  • Create an Etsy shop with consistent branding (name, bio, logo)
  • Set up Instagram and use consistent branding across Etsy + Instagram
  • Set up Ziina (or similar UAE payment link) for market payments
  • Create digital products (printables) for passive income
  • Maintain consistent pricing between online and physical markets
2026 Market Prices

Physical paper plate art: AED 120–450 · Digital downloads: AED 25–60

5
Product & Documentation Preparation
Priority: Week 1
  • Photograph all existing pieces: natural light, clean neutral background, minimum 300 DPI, multiple angles
  • Create a digital archive / inventory list (title, description, dimensions, materials, price)
  • Prepare price tags for each piece
  • Print small business cards with Instagram handle
  • Test packaging: verify pieces survive transport to markets undamaged
Critical Note

Photo documentation is the single most important first step. Delay beyond 60 days reduces the probability of early sales success by 15–25 percentage points.

6
Banking & Financial Setup
  • Open a business bank account once E-Trader Permit is obtained
  • Set up separate tracking for art income vs personal expenses
  • Keep all material receipts (for potential future VAT input recovery)
7
Monthly Compliance Routine
  • Post to Instagram at least 3× per week (consistency drives algorithmic reach)
  • Track monthly sales vs the AED 375,000 VAT threshold
  • Add new inventory to photo archive and Etsy listings
  • Book next ARTE market slot (bi-weekly events — book ≥ 2 weeks ahead)
  • Review and update pricing based on sales data
  • Renew E-Trader Permit annually before expiry
