The Artist

Arpine Akobian

Working from Dubai, Arpine creates bold, joyful pieces in the folk-naive tradition — flat colours, expressive black outlines, playful animals, and intricate decorative borders that reference Eastern European and Armenian folk-art heritage.

Her signature: a teal-blue background, cats and birds sharing the scene, red flowers with golden centres, and polka-dot borders that frame each composition with rhythm.

Visual Language

A distinctive style

Every element is intentional — colour, creature, and curve.

🐱
Folk Creatures
Cats, birds, and florals rendered with bold outlines and flat colour fields — no shading, all character.
🌊
Teal Ground
A vivid #00CED1 background gives warmth and pools the eye before leading it to the figures.
🌸
Red & Gold Blooms
Seven red flowers with gold centres punctuate the scene — a number echoing folk-tale significance.
⭕
Polka-Dot Border
40–45 white dots on a black outer rim, a yellow-green stripe band, and red dividers — a layered, ceremonial frame.
